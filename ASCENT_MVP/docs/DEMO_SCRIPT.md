# ASCENT — 90–120 Second Hackathon Demo

## 0–8s — Hook

Show landing page.

Say:

> "ASCENT turns your real life into an RPG. Every quest you complete gives you XP, upgrades your character, and rebuilds your world."

## 8–20s — Awakening / auth

Signup/login.

Show:

- player name
- goal selection
- character setup

Keep moving; do not linger on forms.

## 20–35s — System home

Show:

- E-rank
- level 1 or low level
- XP bar
- character/world
- 2–3 daily quests
- stats
- Essence
- streak

Say:

> "This isn't a to-do list. The System is tracking progression."

## 35–50s — Complete first quest

Click COMPLETE.

Show:

- reward particles
- `+XP`
- stat increase
- Essence increase
- streak update

## 50–68s — Level up

Trigger a milestone with seeded/demo-friendly state if necessary, but the underlying value must be persisted by the backend.

Show:

- `LEVEL UP`
- aura pulse
- XP bar
- character evolution
- world landmark appears

Say:

> "Leveling isn't just a number—the world changes with the player."

## 68–80s — Inventory/reward

Open inventory.

Show one unlocked item / aura.

## 80–95s — Persistence proof

Refresh browser.

Show:

- same user
- same level
- same Essence
- same quest completion / world state

Say:

> "Everything you just saw is stored in a real backend, not localStorage."

## 95–110s — Close

Show evolved System home.

Final line:

> "ASCENT: Your life. Your quests. Your evolution."

## Recording rules

- Keep the final video within the brief's 90–180 second limit.
- Keep file under 100MB.
- Use a public link or repository-hosted video accessible without login.
- Avoid showing API keys or environment variables.
