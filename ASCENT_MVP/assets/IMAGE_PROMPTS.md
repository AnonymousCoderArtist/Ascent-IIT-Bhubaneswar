# ASCENT — FINAL IMAGE GENERATION PACK v3

## 0. Visual contract

ASCENT is an original **real-life RPG**. The visual language can evoke premium dark-fantasy game systems and anime-inspired progression, but every asset must be original and must not reproduce copyrighted characters, franchise UI, screenshots, logos, costumes, or weapons.

**Palette:** near-black `#07080B`, ivory `#F4F4F0`, violet `#8B5CF6`, electric blue `#4F8CFF`, restrained success green, rare warning red.

**Feel:** mysterious, premium, cinematic, tactile, elegant, not RGB-gamer, not generic SaaS.

**Global negative prompt:** copyrighted character, existing anime franchise character, game screenshot, copied UI, recognizable franchise costume, recognizable franchise weapon, logo, watermark, readable text, subtitle, cheap mobile game aesthetic, rainbow neon, plastic 3D render, photorealistic celebrity, deformed anatomy, extra limbs, extra fingers, muddy silhouette, noisy composition, oversaturated glow.

# 1. ASSET RULES — IMPORTANT

## Transparent assets — real alpha background

Use **real transparent PNG** with alpha. Do not paint a checkerboard into the image.

Generate transparent:

- all complete character illustrations
- aura / halo / XP VFX
- inventory objects
- small achievement icons

## Full-background assets

Use normal JPG/WebP/PNG with a complete environment for:

- landing hero
- awakening scene
- Level 1 world
- Level 5 world
- Level 10 world
- Level 15 world
- Level 20 world
- Level 30 world
- level-up background

## Recommended dimensions

- Characters: **1536×2048 PNG**, transparent
- World scenes: **2560×1440**, 16:9 master; export WebP for production
- VFX: **1024×1024 PNG**, transparent
- Inventory icons: **1024×1024 PNG**, transparent

## Character consistency

Use the same fictional protagonist identity in every character prompt. Keep face, hair silhouette, body proportions, skin tone, camera angle, framing, lighting direction and overall costume language stable. The player must look like **one person evolving**, not seven unrelated characters.
